Why this one
------------
Mobile screens made by the generation agent were built like desktop pages (app name repeated under
the host header, a centred "back" row in the middle of the content, one big card for everything).
The agent needs the platform conventions that people on iPhone and Android expect. This pack turns
the first-party sources (Material Design 3 と Android の指針: developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines,
m3.material.io) into rules with an impact level, is MIT-licensed, plain Markdown (no scripts, no
network) and is the most-installed pack that covers both iOS and Android (see
docs/_research/2026-11-26-mobile-uiux-skills.md).
